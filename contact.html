FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions families ask us most often.

What age groups do you treat?
We treat patients of all ages — from toddlers as young as 12 months to seniors. We have dedicated programs for every life stage.
How do I know if my child needs speech therapy?
Common signs include: not speaking by age 2, difficulty pronouncing words, stammering, not following simple instructions, or limited vocabulary for their age. When in doubt, an early assessment never hurts!
Do you offer home therapy sessions?
Yes! We offer home therapy on request. This is especially useful for patients with mobility challenges or young children who feel more comfortable at home.
How many sessions will my child need?
Every patient is different. After the initial assessment, our therapist will give you a realistic estimate based on severity and goals. Most patients see improvement within 4–8 weeks of regular sessions.
What are your clinic timings?
We are open Monday to Friday 9 AM – 8 PM and Saturday 9 AM – 2 PM. We are closed on Sundays. Please WhatsApp or call ahead to book your slot.
